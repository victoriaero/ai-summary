Academic completion and graduation for cisgender students are primarily influenced by pre-college preparation, school belonging, parental background, and financial resources.
## Key Factors Influencing Cisgender Student Graduation

* Pre-College Academic Preparation: High school grades, standardized test scores, and foundational skills strongly predict retention and degree completion, particularly in rigorous fields like STEM. [1] 
* School Belonging and Environment: Feeling connected to the school community and supported by peers significantly increases the odds of academic achievement and continuation for cisgender students. [2, 3] 
* Teacher Expectations: High expectations and encouragement from educators serve as strong motivational drivers for student success and graduation. [2] 
* Parental Background: Family socioeconomic status, parental education levels, and family stability heavily influence educational attainment and college completion gaps. [4, 5] 
* Financial Support: Access to family resources, personal savings, grants, scholarships, and manageable loan structures determines whether students can sustain enrollment through graduation. [6]
