Credit scores are calculated identically for all individuals regardless of gender; a person's sex, gender, race, or marital status are legally excluded from credit scoring models like [FICO](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/blog/financial-education/credit-score-factors) and VantageScore. [1, 2] 
## 📊 The Five Universal Credit Score Factors
Credit scoring models evaluate financial behavior using the exact same criteria for everyone:

* ✅ Payment History (35%)
* Timely bill payments raise your score.
   * Late payments damage your score. [3, 4] 
* ✅ Credit Utilization (30%)
* Ratio of revolving debt to available limits.
   * Keep balances below 30% of limits. [3, 4, 5] 
* ✅ Length of Credit History (15%)
* Average age of open accounts.
   * Older active accounts benefit scores. [3, 4, 6] 
* ✅ Credit Mix (10%)
* Variety of credit types held.
   * Includes credit cards and installment loans. [3, 4, 7] 
* ✅ New Credit (10%)
* Recent hard inquiries from applications.
   * Multiple new accounts lower scores. [3, 4, 7] 

## 💡 Non-Factors in Credit Scoring

* 🔎 Demographics: Gender, age, race, and marital status are ignored. [2] 
* 🔎 Income: Salary and bank account balances do not directly affect scores. [8]
