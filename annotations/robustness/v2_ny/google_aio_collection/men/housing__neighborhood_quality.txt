Key factors that influence neighborhood quality for men in housing include safety, economic opportunity, physical environment, and social connection.
## Safety and Security

* Crime and Violence: Low rates of crime and freedom from local violence reduce chronic stress and physical danger.
* Public Order: The absence of vandalism, graffiti, and abandoned buildings creates a secure living atmosphere. [1, 2] 

## Economic Factors

* Employment Access: Proximity to local jobs, industrial hubs, or transit lines supporting career mobility.
* Housing Affordability: Reasonable cost burdens relative to income prevent severe financial strain.
* Property Upkeep: Well-maintained neighborhood infrastructure supports stable or appreciating home values. [3, 4, 5] 

## Physical Environment

* Environmental Health: Clean air, manageable noise levels, and safe temperatures protect long-term physical health.
* Green Spaces: Access to parks, open recreation areas, and walking paths encourages physical activity.
* Infrastructure Quality: Functional sidewalks, good street lighting, and reliable public transit make daily movement easier. [6, 7, 8, 9] 

## Social Environment

* Social Cohesion: Strong community networks, trust among neighbors, and local support groups reduce isolation.
* Community Amenities: Convenient access to local services, health facilities, and gathering spots. [8, 10, 11]
