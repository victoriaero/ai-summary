Key factors influencing neighborhood quality for residents generally include safety, proximity to daily necessities, school district quality, access to outdoor spaces, commute options, and environmental cleanliness. [1, 2, 3, 4] 
## Core Neighborhood Quality Factors

* Safety and Security: Low crime rates, active community policing, well-lit streets, and reliable emergency services define a secure living environment. [1, 2] 
* Nearby Necessities: Convenient access to grocery stores, pharmacies, healthcare facilities, and childcare services reduces daily friction. [1] 
* Schools and Education: High test-scoring schools, favorable teacher-student ratios, and robust extracurricular programs drive desirability and property value. [1, 2] 
* Transportation and Walkability: Reliable public transit options, connected sidewalks, bike lanes, and manageable commuting logistics improve overall mobility. [1, 2, 4] 
* Green and Open Spaces: Well-maintained parks, walking trails, and recreational facilities support physical health and community engagement. [1, 4] 
* Environmental Health: Clean air and water, minimal noise pollution, effective waste management, and clean, well-kept streets create a pleasant setting. [3, 4] 
* Social Cohesion: Strong neighborhood networks, friendly neighbors, active community events, and collective efficacy build a supportive social fabric. [1, 5]
