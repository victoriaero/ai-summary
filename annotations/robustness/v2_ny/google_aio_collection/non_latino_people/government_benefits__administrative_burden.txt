AI OVERVIEW TEXT
============================================================

Administrative burden for non-Latino (including White and other non-Hispanic) people in accessing government benefits is driven by three main types of costs as classified by policy scholars: learning costs, compliance costs, and psychological costs. [1] (/goto?url=CAESYQHrOzAVo5d-qyPlJtVB6mQj8TyIfX1IXj3QK72CII8pmbPMBxoRcpssSahIxMmGUS5QMGvacWxwm8BdWHeC12yUJRCNYUTlJ18iYV4TzzNfAEUvIgr6GOkQV1qglBM6_nc)
Types of Administrative Burdens
• Learning Costs: The time and effort required to find out about a program, check eligibility criteria, and understand how to apply for benefits like SNAP or Medicaid. Research indicates that non-Hispanic White adults frequently report navigating these informational requirements as significant hurdles. [1] (/goto?url=CAESwgEB6zswFQ5Ip6REy8Xae1X5ZRlm18x4xLrm1m1JgZWIYVhVfUqVDDtqn1BM-aPlanLM0rZJjqbQLd8OlcniFYCDtH3PrBukpKgL908SUwNRgmxK42Ll6T8o1P8Yl8lQsREuGkJQJ1TEKyQyfkP82AlVxaHYvi5OCJCxPt8XwSlI4TZscXxHbBG7TeqzLCoV5jLu2qaxN0p7CsKaaXBJ-gDKW06cAuVLidMmLt_ZFqbo0SMwOScKyYVPZhZDSI0yJz0Xew), [2] (/goto?url=CAESYQHrOzAVo5d-qyPlJtVB6mQj8TyIfX1IXj3QK72CII8pmbPMBxoRcpssSahIxMmGUS5QMGvacWxwm8BdWHeC12yUJRCNYUTlJ18iYV4TzzNfAEUvIgr6GOkQV1qglBM6_nc)
• Compliance Costs: The operational tasks involved in submitting documentation, completing enrollment or re-enrollment paperwork, and responding to ongoing agency demands. Procedural rules, verification steps, and recertification intervals directly shape these burdens. [1] (/goto?url=CAESagHrOzAVeUstVmRP3lX80ttssl8jYwcryO4dS9mu9lCcly0ynkEnY79J0SUN2QoEUzTYCBV5jqcxIEoDK6e9o6k_ey6pOJwAEBZDIxobFGlodrkfVOnXb1i52-XwWzI0IxuQTPpCMib2gw0), [2] (/goto?url=CAESYQHrOzAVo5d-qyPlJtVB6mQj8TyIfX1IXj3QK72CII8pmbPMBxoRcpssSahIxMmGUS5QMGvacWxwm8BdWHeC12yUJRCNYUTlJ18iYV4TzzNfAEUvIgr6GOkQV1qglBM6_nc)
• Psychological Costs: The stress, loss of personal autonomy, and social stigma associated with participating in public assistance programs. [1] (/goto?url=CAESYQHrOzAVo5d-qyPlJtVB6mQj8TyIfX1IXj3QK72CII8pmbPMBxoRcpssSahIxMmGUS5QMGvacWxwm8BdWHeC12yUJRCNYUTlJ18iYV4TzzNfAEUvIgr6GOkQV1qglBM6_nc)
Broader Influencing Factors
• Program Design and Policy Choices: Macro-level policy choices—such as stricter eligibility checks, mandatory reporting intervals, and enrollment churn mechanisms—increase the overall friction of retaining benefits like Medicaid, CHIP, or TANF. [1] (/goto?url=CAESagHrOzAVeUstVmRP3lX80ttssl8jYwcryO4dS9mu9lCcly0ynkEnY79J0SUN2QoEUzTYCBV5jqcxIEoDK6e9o6k_ey6pOJwAEBZDIxobFGlodrkfVOnXb1i52-XwWzI0IxuQTPpCMib2gw0)
• State-Level Implementation: State policies and decentralized administrative practices heavily influence the scale of these burdens. Studies on income-support programs show that state-level political environments and operational choices directly alter the time and material resources applicants must expend.
