Credit scores are determined entirely by financial behavior and credit history, meaning sexual orientation (such as being heterosexual) has no influence or bearing on a credit score. [1] 
## ✅ The Five Core Factors Influencing Credit Scores
Credit scoring models like [FICO](https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/whats-in-your-credit-score) and VantageScore evaluate everyone using the same financial metrics: [1] 

* ## 📊 Payment History (35%)
* Paying bills on time
   * Avoiding late payments past 30 days
   * Keeping accounts out of collections [2] 
* ## 📈 Amounts Owed / Credit Utilization (30%)
* Total debt relative to limits
   * Keeping credit card utilization below 30%
   * Aimed ideally under 10% for top scores [2, 3] 
* ## 🗓 Length of Credit History (15%)
* Age of oldest and newest accounts
   * Average age of all accounts combined [2] 
* ## 📌 Credit Mix (10%)
* Balancing installment loans (e.g., auto, mortgage)
   * Balancing revolving credit (e.g., credit cards) [2] 
* ## 🔎 New Credit (10%)
* Managing hard inquiries from new applications
   * Spacing out applications to avoid clustered risk [2] 

## ⚠️ Factors Excluded by Law
Under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) in the United States, credit scoring models are legally prohibited from considering: [1] 

* Sexual orientation or marital status
* Race, color, religion, or national origin
* Sex or age (beyond the legal age of contract)
* Income or employment status [1]
