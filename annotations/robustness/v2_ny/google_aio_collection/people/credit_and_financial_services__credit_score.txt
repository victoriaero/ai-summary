Five key factors influence standard credit scores like FICO and VantageScore, regardless of whether an individual works in financial services. [1, 2] 
## ✅ Payment History (35%)

* Track record of paying past bills on time. [3] 
* Includes late or missed payments on credit cards and loans. [4] 
* Most heavily weighted factor in credit calculations. [5] 

## 📊 Amounts Owed / Credit Utilization (30%)

* Ratio of current revolving debt to total available credit limit.
* Recommended to keep utilization below 30%.
* Reflects how dependent you are on non-cash credit. [6, 7] 

## 🗓 Length of Credit History (15%)

* Average age of all open and closed credit accounts. [6] 
* Age of the oldest and newest individual accounts. [6] 
* Longer track records demonstrate sustained credit management. [7] 

## 📌 Credit Mix (10%)

* Variety of credit types active on your report. [8] 
* Includes credit cards, installment loans, and mortgages. [7] 
* Shows experience handling diverse borrowing structures. [3] 

## ⚠️ New Credit (10%)

* Number of recently opened credit accounts.
* Frequency of hard inquiries from new credit applications.
* Rapid applications signal elevated financial risk to lenders. [3]
