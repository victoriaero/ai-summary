Credit scores for people with disabilities are calculated using the exact same standard mathematical factors as everyone else, but socioeconomic barriers and income constraints heavily influence these underlying inputs. [1, 2] 
## 📊 Core Credit Score Factors
Standard credit scoring models (like FICO and VantageScore) do not ask about or track disability status. They rely strictly on five financial behavior categories: [2] 

* 💳 Payment History (35%): Timely payment of credit cards, loans, and reported bills. [2] 
* 📉 Amounts Owed / Credit Utilization (30%): The percentage of available revolving credit being used. [2, 3] 
* 🗓 Length of Credit History (15%): How long credit accounts have been open. [2] 
* 🧩 Credit Mix (10%): A combination of installment loans and revolving accounts. [2] 
* 📌 New Credit (10%): Recent hard inquiries and newly opened accounts. [2] 

## ⚠️ Indirect Influencing Factors for People with Disabilities
While the scoring formula is blind to disability, systemic and situational factors heavily skew these categories for disabled individuals: [1] 

* 💸 Fixed or Lower Incomes: Limited or fixed income from SSDI (Social Security Disability Insurance) or SSI (Supplemental Security Income) makes maintaining low credit utilization or recovering from unexpected medical debt harder.
* 📁 "Thin" Credit Files: Reduced labor force attachment or reliance on cash/debit can lead to insufficient traditional credit history to generate a standard score. [1] 
* 🩺 Medical Debt and Shocks: High out-of-pocket healthcare expenses can result in missed payments or collections that severely damage payment history. [1] 
* 🔄 Alternative Financial Services: Reliance on high-cost alternative credit (like payday or title loans) often fails to build positive traditional credit history while increasing default risk. [4, 5] 

## 💡 Strategies to Build and Protect Credit

* ✅ Report Alternative Data: Use tools like Experian Boost to safely incorporate utility or rental payment data.
* ✅ Secured Credit Cards: Open a deposit-backed secured card to establish a positive payment history with minimal risk.
* ✅ Authorized User Accounts: Become an authorized user on a trusted family member's clean credit account.
* ✅ Credit-Builder Loans: Utilize specialized small-installment loans designed strictly to report on-time payments to major bureaus. [1, 6, 7, 8]
