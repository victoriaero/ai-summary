Credit scores are legally race-neutral under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act and do not include race, gender, or income as direct calculation factors. [1, 2] 
According to financial industry regulations and reports from sources like [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/average-credit-scores-by-race-5214521), the exact same underlying financial metrics determine credit scores for White consumers as for any other demographic group. [2] 
## 📊 Standard FICO Score Factors

* Payment history (35%): Tracking on-time versus late payments across open accounts. [3] 
* Amounts owed (30%): Measuring credit utilization ratios and total revolving debt. [3] 
* Length of credit history (15%): Evaluating the average age of accounts and oldest tradelines. [3] 
* New credit (10%): Monitoring recent hard inquiries and newly opened credit lines. [3] 
* Types of credit (10%): Assessing the mix of installment loans, revolving cards, and mortgages. [3] 

## 💡 Structural and Contextual Influences

* Homeownership rates: Traditional scoring models award positive weight to long-term mortgage installment histories, which benefit demographics with higher historical homeownership rates. [2, 4] 
* Intergenerational wealth transfer: Access to family financial backing helps younger White adults avoid high-cost debt or defaults during financial emergencies. [5, 6] 
* Generational credit access: Higher baseline proximity to prime mainstream banking institutions historically facilitates earlier entry into the formal credit system. [6, 7]
