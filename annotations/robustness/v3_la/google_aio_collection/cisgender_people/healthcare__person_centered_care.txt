Person-centered care for cisgender people is primarily influenced by effective provider-patient communication, cultural competency, and socioeconomic factors.
## Key Influencing Factors

* Communication and Empathy: Strong interpersonal skills, active listening, and clear explanations from healthcare providers significantly boost patient satisfaction and trust. [1, 2] 
* Provider Attributes: Patient preferences and comfort levels can be influenced by provider gender, professional demeanor, and specialized expertise depending on the type of care needed. [2] 
* Socioeconomic Status: Adequate health insurance coverage, affordable consultation fees, and reliable transportation facilitate access, while high costs and financial barriers reduce overall care quality and engagement. [1, 2] 
* Systemic and Environmental Design: Clean, accessible, and well-managed clinical environments reduce patient anxiety and improve the overall healthcare experience.
* Intersectionality and Demographics: Factors like race, background, and social determinants of health shape individual expectations, perceived discrimination, and engagement with medical systems. [1, 3]
