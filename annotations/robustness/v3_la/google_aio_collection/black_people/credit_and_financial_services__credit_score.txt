While credit scoring models like FICO and VantageScore are legally race-neutral and do not explicitly include race as a factor, systemic, economic, and structural disparities heavily influence the underlying data that shape credit scores for Black consumers. [1, 2, 3] 
## 📊 Structural & Economic Drivers

* Racial Wealth Gap: Lower median household wealth reduces the safety net available during financial emergencies. [4] 
* Income Disparities: Disproportionate wage gaps lead to tighter household budgets, increasing default risks. [4, 5] 
* Student Loan Burdens: Black college graduates often carry significantly higher student loan debt than white peers. [6] 
* Predatory & Subprime Lending: Under-resourced communities face higher exposure to fringe lenders and subprime terms. [7] 

## 🗒️ Scoring Model Mechanics

* Exclusion of Rental and Utility Data: Traditional models reward mortgages while historically omitting timely rent and utility payments. [8] 
* Payment History Weight (35%): Unstable income or medical emergencies can quickly trigger delinquencies that disproportionately impact lower-wealth households. [9] 
* Credit Mix & Utilization (30%): Limited access to traditional, prime revolving credit lines restricts optimal credit mix and utilization ratios. [10]
