Credit scores are primarily influenced by five key financial behavior categories, led by [Payment History](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/credit-education/score-basics/what-affects-your-credit-scores/) at 35% and amounts owed at 30%. [1] 
## ✅ The Five Core Credit Score Factors

* ## 🗓️ Payment History (35%)
* Record of paying past bills on time.
   * Late payments, collections, and bankruptcies lower scores. [2, 3] 
* ## 📊 Amounts Owed / Utilization (30%)
* Portion of total available credit currently used.
   * Keeping utilization below 30% helps scores. [3, 4] 
* ## 📅 Length of Credit History (15%)
* Average age of all open credit accounts.
   * Older active accounts benefit your overall score. [4, 5] 
* ## 📌 Credit Mix (10%)
* Variety of credit types like cards and installment loans.
   * Managing multiple credit forms shows responsibility. [3, 6] 
* ## 🔎 New Credit (10%)
* Recent applications and hard credit inquiries.
   * Multiple requests in a short window signal risk. [4, 6] 

## 💡 What Does Not Affect Your Credit Score

* Income, employment status, or job title.
* Age, race, gender, or marital status.
* Utility or phone bills—unless reported via programs like Experian Boost. [1, 7]
