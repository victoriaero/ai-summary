Credit scores are determined by financial behavior and credit management, and gender does not influence credit scores. Under U.S. law (such as the Equal Credit Opportunity Act), scoring models like FICO and VantageScore do not use gender, race, marital status, or income when calculating your score.
The same five core financial factors apply to everyone:
• Payment History (35%): Your track record of paying bills on time. Late payments, collections, or defaults lower your score.
• Amounts Owed / Credit Utilization (30%): How much of your available credit limit you are currently using. Keeping balances below 30% (ideally under 10%) helps your score.
• Length of Credit History (15%): How long your credit accounts have been open and active. A longer history builds a higher score.
• Credit Mix (10%): Having a variety of account types, such as credit cards, installment loans, or a mortgage.
• New Credit / Inquiries (10%): Opening multiple new accounts or getting many hard inquiries in a short time can signal risk and lower your score.
