Neighborhood quality for people without disabilities depends on core physical, economic, social, and environmental factors that shape livability, safety, and daily convenience. [1, 2, 3] 
## 1. Safety and Security

* Crime rates: Lower rates of violent and property crime directly drive up neighborhood desirability.
* Law enforcement and emergency services: Quick response times from police, fire departments, and medical services provide peace of mind.
* Physical order: Clean streets, absence of graffiti or vandalism, and well-maintained properties signal a secure environment. [2, 4] 

## 2. Affordability and Housing Market

* Housing costs: Reasonable median home prices, low rental costs, and a balanced supply of available housing dictate access.
* Tenure mix: A healthy mix of owned homes and rental properties supports long-term residential stability.
* Property maintenance: Solid structural upkeep prevents hazards like mold, lead, or poor insulation. [2, 5, 6] 

## 3. Transportation and Mobility

* Public transit: Reliable access to buses, trains, or commuter lines reduces travel friction.
* Walkability and infrastructure: Continuous sidewalks, well-lit streets, and safe crosswalks make walking practical.
* Road access: Proximity to major highways, parking availability, and low traffic congestion improve daily commutes. [2, 7] 

## 4. Education and Local Amenities

* School quality: High-performing local K-12 school ratings, active teacher support, and extracurricular programs heavily influence families.
* Parks and recreation: Close access to green spaces, playgrounds, walking trails, and community centers enhances lifestyle quality.
* Commercial convenience: Proximity to grocery stores, restaurants, healthcare clinics, and daily services saves time. [1, 2, 7] 

## 5. Social and Environmental Health

* Social cohesion and collective efficacy: Neighbors who know, trust, and look out for one another build a strong sense of community.
* Environmental quality: Clean air, low noise pollution from traffic or industry, and comfortable local climates protect long-term health. [1, 3, 4]
