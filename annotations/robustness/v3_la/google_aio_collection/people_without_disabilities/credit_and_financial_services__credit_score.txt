Credit scores are calculated using five core financial factors from your credit reports, regardless of disability status.
📅 1. Payment History
• Tracks whether you pay your bills.
• On-time payments build higher scores.
• Late payments or defaults lower scores.
• Weight: ~35% (FICO) to 40% (VantageScore).
📊 2. Credit Utilization
• Ratio of current debt to credit limits.
• Keep usage under 30% (ideally under 10%).
• High balances signal financial overextension.
• Weight: ~30% (FICO) to 20% (VantageScore).
🗓 3. Length of Credit History
• Average age of all open and closed accounts.
• Older accounts show long-term stability.
• Keeping old accounts active helps your score.
• Weight: ~15% (FICO) to 21% (VantageScore).
💡 4. Credit Mix
• Variety of credit types managed.
• Includes credit cards, retail, and installment loans.
• Showing diverse credit management raises scores.
• Weight: ~10% (FICO) to 11% (VantageScore).
📌 5. Recent Credit Activity
• Tracks newly opened accounts.
• Hard inquiries occur when applying for new credit.
• Multiple new applications in a short time lower scores.
• Weight: ~10% (FICO) to 5% (VantageScore).
