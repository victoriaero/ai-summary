Credit scores are gender-neutral and calculated using the exact same financial factors for everyone, regardless of gender. [1] 
## ✅ The 5 Core Credit Score Factors

* Payment History (35%): On-time bill payments. [2] 
* Credit Utilization (30%): Debt owed versus available limits. [3] 
* Length of Credit History (15%): Age of open accounts. [2] 
* Credit Mix (10%): Variety of credit types. [2] 
* New Credit (10%): Recent credit applications and inquiries. [2] 

## 🚫 Excluded Personal Data

* Gender: Not used in scoring models. [1] 
* Race or Ethnicity: Legally prohibited from inclusion. [1] 
* Marital Status: Excluded from credit calculations. [1] 
* Income and Employment: Not part of credit bureau scoring algorithms. [1] 

## 💡 Financial Action Plan

* Automate payments to protect your payment history.
* Keep utilization low by staying under 30% of your credit limits.
* Limit new inquiries by spacing out credit applications. [2, 3]
