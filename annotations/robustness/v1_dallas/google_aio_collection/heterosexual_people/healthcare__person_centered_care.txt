Person-centered care (PCC) for heterosexual individuals in healthcare is shaped by a combination of interpersonal communication, individual psychosocial factors, and systemic healthcare environments. Because heterosexual identity aligns with societal norms, the healthcare focus often shifts away from orientation-based validation toward broader determinants of health and relational dynamics. [1, 2, 3, 4] 
## Key Influencing Factors

* Patient-Provider Communication: Active dialogue, empathy, and shared decision-making strongly drive patient satisfaction and effective care alignment. [1, 5] 
* Gender Concordance and Preferences: The biological sex or perceived gender of the healthcare provider can influence comfort levels, empathy perception, and willingness to disclose intimate health details. [2, 5] 
* Psychosocial and Lifestyle Context: A patient's mental health, emotional stress, socioeconomic status, cultural background, and family responsibilities directly impact how well care plans are understood and executed. [6, 7] 
* Structural and Environmental Integration: Access to affordable care, insurance coverage, private clinical spaces, and digital health tools significantly affect safety, engagement, and overall health outcomes. [4, 5, 8]
