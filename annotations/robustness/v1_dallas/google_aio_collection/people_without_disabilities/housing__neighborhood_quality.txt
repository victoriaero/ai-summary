## Key Factors of Neighborhood Quality

* Safety and Security: Low crime rates, active neighborhood watch programs, good street lighting, and reliable emergency response services ensure everyday livability. [1, 2] 
* School Districts: High test scores, favorable teacher-student ratios, and rich academic or extracurricular programs drive neighborhood desirability and property value. [1, 2] 
* Nearby Necessities: Convenient access to grocery stores, pharmacies, healthcare providers, and childcare centers reduces daily commute burdens. [1] 
* Transportation and Commute: Proximity to major employment centers, reliable public transit options, and safe walking or biking paths define urban mobility. [1, 2] 
* Outdoor and Recreational Spaces: Well-maintained parks, walking trails, and green open spaces encourage physical activity and community connection. [1, 3] 
* Environmental Health: Clean air, minimal noise pollution, and well-kept streets with effective waste management support long-term physical and mental well-being. [4] 
* Social Cohesion: Strong community networks, neighborly support, and active civic or cultural institutions create a sense of belonging and collective safety. [5, 6]
