Credit scores are calculated using the exact same core financial criteria for all individuals, regardless of disability status.
## 📊 The 5 Core Credit Score Factors

* ## 📌 Payment History (35%–40%)
* On-time bill payments
   * Late or missed payments
   * Public records (bankruptcies, collections) [1, 2, 3, 4] 
* ## 📌 Credit Utilization (20%–30%)
* Ratio of revolving debt to credit limit
   * Recommended keeping usage below 30%
   * Total combined outstanding balances [2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9] 
* ## 📌 Length of Credit History (15%–21%)
* Age of oldest account
   * Age of newest account
   * Average age of all accounts [2, 3] 
* ## 📌 Credit Mix (10%–11%)
* Combination of credit cards
   * Installment loans (auto, student)
   * Mortgages [2, 3, 6, 7] 
* ## 📌 New Credit & Recent Activity (5%–10%)
* Hard credit inquiries
   * Number of newly opened accounts [2, 3, 4, 7] 

## 💡 Excluded Factors
Federal law strictly prohibits credit scoring models and credit reports from factoring in demographic or personal traits, including:

* Race, ethnicity, or gender
* Income level, employment, or receipt of public assistance (like SSI/SSDI)
* Address, age, or marital status [1, 4]
