Standard credit scores like FICO and VantageScore use the exact same race-neutral mathematical factors for all consumers regardless of race or ethnicity. [1, 2] 
## 📊 Core Scoring Factors
According to [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/average-credit-scores-by-race-5214521), credit-scoring models rely on five universal components:

* ✅ Payment history (35%): Tracking on-time versus late payments. [1, 3] 
* ✅ Credit utilization (30%): Ratio of revolving debt to total available limits. [1, 3] 
* ✅ Length of credit history (15%): Age of oldest and average accounts. [1, 3] 
* ✅ New credit (10%): Frequency of recent inquiries and newly opened accounts. [1, 3] 
* ✅ Credit mix (10%): Balance of installment loans and revolving credit cards. [1, 3] 

## ⚠️ Excluded Personal Demographics

* 📌 Race, ethnicity, and national origin are legally prohibited and omitted from scoring calculations. [2, 4] 
* 📌 Age, gender, marital status, salary, and residential location are also excluded from standard credit-scoring formulas. [4, 5]
