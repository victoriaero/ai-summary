The five primary factors that influence standard credit scores like FICO® and VantageScore® are payment history, amounts owed, length of credit history, credit mix, and new credit. [1, 2] 
## 📅 35% Payment History

* Track record of paying bills on time.
* Includes late payments, collections, and bankruptcies.
* Single missed payment significantly lowers scores. [3, 4] 

## 📊 30% Amounts Owed (Credit Utilization)

* Ratio of revolving debt to available credit limits. [4, 5] 
* Optimal target is keeping utilization at or below 30%. [6] 
* High balances relative to limits hurt scores. [7] 

## ⏱️ 15% Length of Credit History

* Average age of open credit accounts.
* Older accounts demonstrate long-term stability.
* Keeping old, inactive accounts open preserves history. [4, 5, 6] 

## 🗂️ 💡 10% Credit Mix

* Variety of account types managed responsibly.
* Includes credit cards, installment loans, and mortgages.
* Shows experience with diverse credit products. [4, 5, 6] 

## 🔎 10% New Credit

* Frequency of recent credit applications and inquiries. [4, 6] 
* Multiple hard inquiries raise risk flags for lenders. [4] 
* Opening many accounts rapidly depresses average account age. [4, 8]
