AI OVERVIEW TEXT
============================================================

Income, financial literacy, and structural costs heavily influence access to banking services.
Economic and Cost Factors
• Income level: Lower-income households often struggle to maintain minimum balance requirements, leading to exclusion or reliance on alternative financial services.
• Bank fees: Maintenance costs, overdraft charges, and insufficient fund fees create financial burdens that disproportionately affect lower-balance consumers.
• Credit scores: Subprime or missing credit scores limit approval for standard banking and lending products.
Knowledge and Trust
• Financial literacy: A lack of financial education prevents consumers from navigating account requirements and avoiding high-fee structures.
• Institutional trust: Skepticism regarding data privacy, security, and traditional banking institutions deters unbanked populations from opening formal accounts.
Institutional and Structural Barriers
• Documentation requirements: Strict identification rules create involuntary barriers for marginalized or unverified individuals.
• Physical infrastructure: Limited branch availability or poor local banking infrastructure reduces face-to-face access in rural or underserved areas.
