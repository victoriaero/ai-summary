Career advancement for transgender people is heavily influenced by workplace climate, social support, and systemic discrimination. [1, 2] 
## Key Influencing Factors

* Workplace Culture and Inclusion: Access to inclusive non-discrimination policies, gender-neutral facilities, and active managerial support directly affect retention and promotion. [1, 3] 
* Social Support and Allyship: Strong networks of supportive co-workers and visible allies reduce workplace anxiety and isolation, helping employees focus on professional goals. [1, 4] 
* Discrimination and Bias: Prejudicial attitudes, harassment, misgendering, and unfair performance evaluations—especially affecting transgender women—create concrete barriers to leadership roles. [1, 5] 
* Mental Health and Safety: Navigating unsupportive environments imposes a heavy psychological toll, draining the energy needed for professional engagement and career growth. [2, 6] 
* Disclosure and Transition Support: Deciding whether and how to transition at work, alongside having a formal transition plan and workplace accommodations, strongly shapes career sustainability. [1, 7]
