Access to credit is primarily influenced by an individual's credit score, credit history, stable income, and debt-to-income ratio.
Key Financial Factors
• Credit Score and History: Lenders review your past payment performance, how much debt you currently owe, and the length of your credit history. A higher score shows lower risk.
• Income and Employment Stability: Consistent earnings prove you have the cash flow to make regular loan payments.
• Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio: Lenders compare your monthly debt payments to your monthly income to see if you can handle extra borrowing.
• Collateral and Assets: Valuable property, savings, or investments can secure a loan, making it easier to get approval.
Institutional and Systemic Factors
• Financial Literacy: Knowing how banks operate, how to read technical terms, and how to prepare tax documents helps people successfully apply for traditional credit.
• Documentation and Verification: Traditional lenders require strict proof of identity, income, and residence, which can block individuals lacking proper paperwork.
• Geographic and Digital Access: Living near a bank branch or having reliable internet and mobile technology determines whether you can easily access modern financial products.
