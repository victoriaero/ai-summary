Hiring decisions for people without disabilities are primarily influenced by standard labor market factors such as professional qualifications, relevant work experience, and general economic conditions.
Core Influencing Factors
• Qualifications and Skills: Employers evaluate candidates based on their educational background, technical certifications, and mastery of specific job-related tasks.
• Work Experience: Past job history, demonstrated performance, and industry-specific tenure heavily drive selection.
• Economic and Industry Trends: Overall labor demand, company growth, and the specific labor supply within an industry dictate hiring volumes and selectivity.
• Cultural and Social Fit: Interpersonal communication skills, alignment with company values, and team dynamics play a major role in final hiring choices.
