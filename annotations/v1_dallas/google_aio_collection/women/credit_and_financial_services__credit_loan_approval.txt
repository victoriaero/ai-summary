AI OVERVIEW TEXT

Credit and loan approval for women is influenced by standard financial metrics alongside systemic and demographic factors that create distinct structural challenges.
Core Financial Factors
-  Credit History & Scores: Payment history and credit utilization heavily dictate approval odds and interest rates.

- Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI): Lenders measure monthly debt obligations against gross income to gauge repayment capacity.
- Income Stability & Employment: Lenders prefer consistent, documented earnings over fluctuating or freelance revenue.
Structural & Systemic Disparities
- The Entrepreneurial Credit Gap: Women-owned businesses face higher denial rates and smaller loan allocations even when controlling for credit score and revenue.
- Asset & Collateral Ownership: Historical wealth and property disparities affect access to secured financing.
- Socioeconomic and Mobility Constraints: Inclusion barriers limit formal credit readiness in developing or marginalized segments.
