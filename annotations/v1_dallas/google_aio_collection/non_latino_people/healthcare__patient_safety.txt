Patient safety in healthcare is influenced by general clinical, systemic, and human factors that apply broadly to non-Latino populations as well as the general population.
Key Factors Influencing Patient Safety
• Communication and Teamwork: Breakdowns in communication among healthcare workers, within medical teams, or between providers and patients lead to medical errors, missed care, and delayed treatments.
• Health Literacy and Engagement: A patient's limited understanding of health conditions, medication instructions, or treatment plans can result in poor adherence or failure to recognize adverse symptoms.
• Provider Fatigue and Burnout: High stress, exhaustion, and cognitive bias among clinical staff reduce diagnostic accuracy and increase the risk of procedural or dosing mistakes.
• Socioeconomic Status and Insurance: Differences in income, education level, and health insurance type affect access to high-quality facilities, continuity of care, and the timely detection of safety risks.
• Systemic and Institutional Practices: Inconsistent safety data collection, understaffing, and organizational oversights within healthcare facilities impact the overall reliability and safety of the care environment.
