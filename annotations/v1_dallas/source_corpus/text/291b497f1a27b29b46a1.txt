The National Women’s Law Center fights for gender justice—in the courts, in public policy, and in our society.
Women in Poverty, State by State
More than one in 10 women aged 18 and older – over 14.8 million – lived in poverty in 2024. Poverty rates were particularly high for Black women (17.8 percent), Latinas (15.2 percent), and Native women (19.4 percent). Nearly one in three (30.6 percent) families headed by single women with children were poor in 2024. Women made up three in five seniors who lived in poverty last year, with the poverty rate for senior women at 10.8 percent.
