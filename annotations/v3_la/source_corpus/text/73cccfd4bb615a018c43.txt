California’s prosperity depends on the Latino workforce. From the Inland Empire, Los Angeles to the Bay Area, and the San Joaquin Valley, Latinos sustain critical industries such as agriculture, construction, manufacturing, education, and social services while driving population and workforce growth. Despite these contributions, Latino workers face barriers that limit economic security and upward mobility.
Sourced from the Latino Data Hub, this series of workforce briefs from LPPI offers a detailed look at demographic trends, employment conditions, and disparities shaping Latino workers in four regions. By providing unique in-depth local insights, the briefs highlight critical areas where attention and action are needed to ensure that workforce strategies are responsive to the realities of the state’s largest labor force.
