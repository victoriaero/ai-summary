Hiring decisions are shaped by a combination of candidate qualifications, organizational needs, and psychological dynamics. [1, 2] 
## 📊 Candidate Qualifications

* Hard skills: Technical knowledge and role-specific certifications.
* Experience: Relevant past work history and achievements.
* Soft skills: Communication clarity, adaptability, and teamwork. [1, 3] 

## 🏢 Organizational Factors

* Company culture: Alignment with team values and workplace norms.
* Budget constraints: Total compensation limits and financial targets.
* Hiring urgency: Speed required to fill an open vacancy. [1, 3, 4, 5] 

## 💡 Psychological & Hidden Factors

* Perceived risk: Choosing candidates seen as easier to onboard.
* Grounded confidence: Calm and authentic communication styles.
* Unconscious bias: Subconscious impressions from first interactions. [2, 6]
