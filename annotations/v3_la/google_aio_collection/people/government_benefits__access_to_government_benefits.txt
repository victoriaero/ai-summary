Access to government benefits is primarily influenced by administrative burdens, eligibility thresholds, and socioeconomic awareness. [1, 2, 3] 
## 🗒 Administrative Burdens

* Paperwork Costs: Long forms and frequent recertification reduce participation. [4] 
* Compliance Steps: In-person interviews and strict documentation create barriers. [1] 
* Psychological Toll: Perceived social stigma discourages eligible applicants. [4] 

## 📊 Eligibility and Policy Rules

* Income Limits: Strict Federal Poverty Level thresholds dictate initial qualification. [3, 5] 
* Work Requirements: Mandatory employment hours condition ongoing receipt. [3] 
* Benefits Cliffs: Small income increases trigger sudden benefit losses. [3] 

## 💡 Knowledge and Resources

* Program Awareness: Misunderstanding eligibility rules limits enrollment. [2, 4] 
* Digital Access: Modern online tools improve application success rates. [5] 
* Funding Caps: Non-entitlement programs face strict annual budget limits. [6]
