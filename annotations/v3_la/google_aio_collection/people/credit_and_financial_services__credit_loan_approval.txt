Lenders evaluate credit and loan approval based on your credit history, debt levels, income stability, and available assets. [1] 
## 📊 Key Factors Influencing Loan Approval

* ## ✅ Credit Score & History
* FICO scores range from 300 to 850, with 670+ considered good.
   * Payment history: Represents 35% of your score; tracks on-time payments.
   * Amounts owed: Represents 30% of your score; measures credit utilization (ideally under 30%).
   * History length: Older accounts show steady reliability. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 
* ## 📊 Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
* Compares monthly debt payments to gross monthly income.
   * Most lenders prefer a DTI ratio below 35% to 50%.
   * Lower DTI proves you have room for a new payment. [1, 3, 6] 
* ## 🗓️ Employment and Income Stability
* Requires proof of steady, verifiable cash flow.
   * Lenders look at your current job tenure and work history.
   * Gaps in employment make income harder to verify. [1, 2, 6, 7] 
* ## 📌 Collateral and Assets
* Secured loans require physical assets like cars or homes.
   * Lenders assess equity value to mitigate default risks.
   * Savings and bank activity show practical money management. [1, 7] 
* ## 🔎 Application Accuracy
* Mismatched names or wrong account numbers cause delays.
   * Inconsistent income figures can trigger application denials. [6]
