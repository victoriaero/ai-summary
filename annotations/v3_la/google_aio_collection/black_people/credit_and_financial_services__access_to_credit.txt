Socioeconomic disparities, geographic banking deserts, and algorithmic credit scoring models heavily influence access to credit for Black people.
📊 Key Factors Influencing Credit Access
• Credit Invisibility and Thin Files
	• About 15% of Black consumers lack sufficient credit history to be scored.
	• Traditional scoring models historically excluded on-time rent and utility payments.
• Geographic Disparities and Bank Deserts
	• Majority-Black neighborhoods experience higher rates of bank branch closures.
	• Residents face higher reliance on alternative, non-reporting financial services like payday lenders.
• The Racial Wealth Gap
	• Lower median intergenerational wealth limits down payments and liquid reserves.
	• Higher vulnerability to emergency debt from medical or employment shocks impairs repayment ratios.
• Algorithmic and Lending Disparities
	• Automated underwriting systems can reflect structural biases from historical data.
	• Black mortgage and small business applicants face higher denial rates even when matching financial profiles.
