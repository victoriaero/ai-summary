Credit and loan approval decisions are based on financial metrics and creditworthiness rather than gender. Lenders evaluate every applicant using standard financial criteria known as the Five Cs of Credit. [1, 2] 
## 💡 Core Approval Factors

* Credit History: Your payment track record and history of managing debt responsibly. [1, 3] 
* Capacity (DTI): Your debt-to-income ratio showing if your income covers monthly payments. [1, 3] 
* Capital: Your savings, investments, and available liquid assets. [1] 
* Collateral: Assets like a car or home used to secure the loan. [1, 3] 
* Conditions: The loan purpose and broader economic environment. [1] 

## 📊 Credit Score Breakdown
Lenders heavily rely on credit scores (such as FICO ranging from 300 to 850), which are calculated using these exact weights: [1, 4] 

* Payment history: 35% of your total score.
* Credit utilization: 30% of your total score.
* Length of credit history: 15% of your total score.
* Credit mix: 10% of your total score.
* New credit inquiries: 10% of your total score. [4, 5]
