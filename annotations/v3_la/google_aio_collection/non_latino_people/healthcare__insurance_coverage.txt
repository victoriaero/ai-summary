Insurance coverage for non-Latino populations is primarily driven by employment status, household income, and geographic location.
## 📊 Economic and Employment Factors

* Income level relative to federal poverty guidelines
* Access to employer-sponsored health insurance plans
* Job type and part-time versus full-time status
* Affordability of monthly premium contributions

## 🗺 Geographic and Policy Factors

* State-level Medicaid expansion decisions [1] 
* Availability of KFF Health Insurance Marketplace subsidies [2] 
* Rural versus urban health provider availability
* Local cost-of-living adjustments for insurance tiers

## 📌 Demographic Characteristics

* Age brackets affecting [Medicare](https://www.medicare.gov) or youth coverage eligibility
* Educational attainment levels
* Family structure and household size dynamics [3, 4, 5]
