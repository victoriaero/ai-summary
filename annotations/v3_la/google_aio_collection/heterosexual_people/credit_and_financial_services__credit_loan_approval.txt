## 📊 Core Financial Factors

* ## 📈 Credit Score and History
* Payment history shows past on-time or missed payments.
   * Credit utilization measures debt relative to credit limits.
   * Length of history reflects long-term credit management experience. [1, 2] 
* ## 📉 Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
* Compares total monthly debt payments to gross monthly income.
   * Lenders prefer lower ratios (typically under 35% to 43%).
   * High DTI indicates limited capacity for new loan payments. [3, 4] 
* ## 💡 Income and Employment Stability
* Verifies steady, recurring earnings to ensure repayment capacity.
   * Evaluates job duration and continuity with current employers. [3, 5] 
* ## 🗓 Assets and Collateral
* Liquid cash flow, savings, and investments indicate financial cushion.
   * Secured loans require physical collateral like a vehicle or home. [6]
