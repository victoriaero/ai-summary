Heterosexual people in healthcare experience insurance coverage influenced by standard socioeconomic, demographic, and regional risk factors rather than sexual orientation-specific disparities. [1, 2] 
## 📋 Key Factors Influencing Coverage

* Employment Status: Employer-sponsored plans form the primary source of private insurance.
* Income Level: Higher household earnings increase the affordability and uptake of comprehensive private plans. [2] 
* Geographic Location: State-level Medicaid expansion policies alter public coverage availability. [2] 
* Age Brackets: Premiums shift by age group under standard risk-rating rules. [3] 
* Family Structure: Marital status affects eligibility for dependent coverage and tax advantages. [4]
