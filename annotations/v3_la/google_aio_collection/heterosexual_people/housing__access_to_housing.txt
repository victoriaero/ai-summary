## Key Factors Influencing Housing Access

* Income and Affordability: Household earnings, job stability, and debt-to-income ratios dictate purchasing power in both rental and real estate markets. [1] 
* Racial and Ethnic Disparities: Historical redlining and ongoing wealth gaps mean Black and Hispanic heterosexual households face higher cost burdens and lower mortgage approval rates compared to white households. [2] 
* Marital and Family Status: Married different-sex couples and dual-income households have significantly higher homeownership rates (around 75% to 79%) compared to single individuals. [3, 4] 
* Credit History and Savings: Access to capital for down payments, credit scores, and financial reserves heavily influence the ability to secure conventional mortgages or competitive rentals.
* Disability Status: Physical or mental health disabilities create distinct barriers to finding accessible and affordable housing units, independent of sexual orientation. [1, 5]
