Hiring decisions for people without disabilities are primarily influenced by professional qualifications, relevant work experience, and standard interview performance. [1] 
## Education and Technical Skills

* Degrees and Certifications: Employers look for specific academic degrees or professional certificates that match the job duties.
* Hard Skills: Technical knowledge, computer proficiency, and specialized training directly relate to job tasks. [2] 

## Work Experience

* Job History: Past employment in similar roles shows a proven track record.
* Tenure: Staying at previous jobs for a solid length of time signals stability and reliability to employers.

## Soft Skills and Communication

* Interpersonal Skills: Clear verbal communication, active listening, and teamwork are vital during interviews.
* Cultural Fit: Employers evaluate how well a candidate shares company values and works with the existing team.

## Smooth Application Processes

* Standard Hiring Steps: Traditional application forms, online portals, and in-person interviews match the everyday design of most workplaces. [3] 
* No Accommodation Needs: Candidates do not require physical or technological adjustments to complete standard assessments, which streamlines the evaluation process. [3]
