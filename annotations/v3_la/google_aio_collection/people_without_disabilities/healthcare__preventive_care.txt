Key factors influencing preventive care utilization for people without disabilities include personal cost concerns, health literacy levels, connection to a primary care provider, and logistical hurdles like scheduling and transportation. [1] 
## Core Influencing Factors

* Cost and Coverage: High out-of-pocket expenses and insurance limitations often cause individuals to delay or skip routine screenings and wellness visits. [1] 
* Health Literacy: A lack of clear, understandable health information impairs a person's ability to navigate and decide on necessary preventive protocols. [1] 
* Primary Care Connection: Having a regular primary care provider strongly increases the likelihood that a patient receives timely vaccinations and routine check-ups. [1] 
* Logistical and Emotional Barriers: Busy work schedules, long travel distances to facilities, fear of bad news, and embarrassment can deter people from completing preventive care. [1] 
* Demographics and Health Status: Age, education level, occupation, and the presence of existing chronic conditions frequently shift how actively individuals seek out health screenings. [2, 3]
