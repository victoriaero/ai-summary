Access to credit for people without disabilities is primarily driven by traditional financial health metrics like credit history, verifiable income, and debt-to-income ratios. Lenders use standardized automated underwriting models to evaluate these standard criteria. [1, 2] 
## 📊 Credit History & Scores

* Payment history tracking past bills
* Total active debt amounts owed
* Length of established credit accounts
* Credit utilization ratio percentages [3] 

## 📈 Income & Employment

* Stable and verifiable employment earnings
* Acceptable debt-to-income (DTI) ratios
* Liquid savings and asset reserves

## 📌 Lending Infrastructure

* Automated risk-scoring algorithms
* Direct access to traditional banks
* Standardized loan approval guidelines [1]
