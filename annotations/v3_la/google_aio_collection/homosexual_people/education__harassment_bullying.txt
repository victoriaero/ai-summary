Harassment and bullying of homosexual and LGBTQ+ individuals in educational settings are driven by a combination of cultural, institutional, and interpersonal factors. [1] 
## Cultural and Ideological Norms

* Heteronormativity and Cisnormativity: Deeply ingrained societal assumptions that heterosexuality and cisgender identities are the default or "normal" standards create an environment where non-conforming individuals are marginalized. [2] 
* Gender Non-Conformity: Students who deviate from traditional gender expression—such as boys perceived as feminine or girls perceived as masculine—face a significantly higher risk of being targeted. [3] 
* Stigma and Prejudice: Rooted biases like homophobia, sexism, and toxic masculinity fuel peer aggression and derogatory language use. [1, 4] 

## Institutional and Environmental Factors

* School Climate and Policies: The absence of comprehensive anti-bullying policies, inclusive curricula, or supportive resources leaves students vulnerable and signals a lack of institutional protection. [5, 6] 
* Staff and Faculty Response: Inconsistent intervention or overt bigotry from teachers and school personnel compound the hostility. Many students do not report incidents because they believe staff will do nothing or fear being "outed" to unsupportive parents. [6, 7, 8, 9] 
* Lack of Support Systems: Schools lacking protective structures—such as Gay-Straight Alliances (GSAs) or accessible mental health professionals—fail to provide safe havens for at-risk youth. [5, 9] 

## Interpersonal and Intersectionality Factors

* Intersection of Identities: Multiple marginalized identities, such as being an LGBTQ+ youth of color or facing socioeconomic disadvantages, compound the risk and severity of victimization.
* Peer Dynamics: Group pressure, a desire to fit in, and aggressive power imbalances drive individual students or groups to use verbal slurs or physical intimidation.
* Cyberbullying: Digital harassment via social media and mobile phones frequently spills over into the physical school environment, making the bullying continuous. [1, 2, 4, 9, 10]
