## 📉 Wage and Wealth Disparities

* Lower earning potential: Gay and bisexual workers often experience a persistent wage gap, earning less per dollar compared to typical non-LGBTQ+ peers. [1, 2]

* Depleted savings: Out-of-pocket costs for healthcare and family formation reduce liquid assets available for down payments. [1, 5]
