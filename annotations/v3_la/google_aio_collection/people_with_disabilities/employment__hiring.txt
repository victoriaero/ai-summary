Hiring for people with disabilities is heavily influenced by employer attitudes, company size and resources, workplace accessibility, and knowledge of accommodations. [1, 2, 3, 4] 
## Key Barriers

* Employer Misconceptions: Many employers mistakenly assume that workers with disabilities are less productive or that providing workplace adjustments is too expensive. In reality, data shows most accommodations cost little to nothing. [1, 5, 6] 
* Lack of Knowledge: A shortage of employer education regarding disability types, legal frameworks (like the Americans with Disabilities Act), and human resource policies creates hesitation during recruitment. [1, 2] 
* Inaccessibility: Physical barriers at worksites (such as missing ramps) and inaccessible digital communication technologies or software prevent candidates from applying or succeeding in roles. [2, 4] 
* Logistical Obstacles: Limited access to reliable, affordable, or accessible public transportation stops many job seekers from reaching traditional workplaces. [4, 7] 
* Social Stigma: Prejudiced attitudes, negative stereotypes, and unsupportive workplace cultures discourage candidates from disclosing needs or applying. [2, 4] 

## Key Facilitators and Influences

* Company Size and Industry: Larger organizations (over 200 employees) are generally more likely to hire people with disabilities because they have dedicated human resource teams and more flexible budgets for accommodations. [1, 3] 
* Business Benefits and Retention: Forward-thinking companies recognize that hiring individuals with disabilities expands talent pools, boosts overall employee morale, and results in higher employee loyalty and retention. [8, 9, 10] 
* Inclusive Policies: Clear, written company policies and active leadership commitment toward diversity and inclusion strongly drive equitable hiring practices forward. [2, 10] 
* Financial Incentives: Tax credits and deductions—such as the Work Opportunity Tax Credit—help offset the financial perceived burden for small and mid-sized businesses. [9]
