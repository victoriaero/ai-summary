AI OVERVIEW TEXT
============================================================

Access to banking for Latino individuals is heavily shaped by documentation requirements, language gaps, and institutional distrust. These barriers result in higher rates of unbanked and underbanked households.
⚠️ Identification and Documentation
• Lack of a Social Security Number (SSN)
• High costs and hurdles to get an ITIN
• Strict bank policies rejecting foreign IDs
• Exclusion from traditional credit scoring models
💡 Language and Culture
• Limited English proficiency in branch locations
• Scarcity of bilingual financial representatives
• Lack of native-language digital onboarding tools
• Cultural preference for cash and community savings
🔎 Trust and History
• Past economic crises in home countries
• Perceived systemic bias in traditional banking
• Reliance on alternative financial services like payday lenders
• Vulnerability to predatory online lending products
📊 Credit and Inclusion Gaps
• Absence of formal U.S. credit history
• Disproportionately higher unbanked and underbanked rates
• High mobile fintech adoption as a workaround
• Lower loan approval rates for small businesses
