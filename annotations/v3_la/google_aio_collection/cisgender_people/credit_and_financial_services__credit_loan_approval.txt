Credit and loan approval decisions for cisgender individuals rely primarily on objective financial metrics, though broader economic and systemic gender disparities can still influence outcomes.
📊 Core Financial Factors
• Credit Score:
	• Payment history on past credit cards or loans.
	• Length of credit history and active accounts.
• Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio:
	• Comparison of monthly debt payments to gross monthly income.
	• High ratios signal overextension and lower approval odds.
• Income & Employment Stability:
	• Consistent documentation of earnings via W-2s or tax returns.
	• Time spent at current employer or residence.
⚖️ Indirect & Systemic Influences
• Gender Wage Gaps:
	• Lower average earnings for women can impact DTI ratios and maximum loan amounts.
• Collateral & Guarantor Demands:
	• Studies show female applicants occasionally face higher demands for guarantors or collateral due to implicit bias.
• Marital Status & History:
	• Divorce or separation can disproportionately affect individual credit profiles and asset ownership.
