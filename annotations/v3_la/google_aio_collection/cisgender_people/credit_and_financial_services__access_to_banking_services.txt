📊 Key Factors Influencing Access
• Income & Employment: Higher earnings and formal salaried jobs directly improve credit approval odds and account ownership.
• Credit History & Collateral: Lenders typically require traditional assets or established borrowing records, disadvantaging informal workers.
• Gender Roles & Family Dynamics: Cisgender women often face systemic hurdles regarding household resource control, caregiving burdens, and lower average earnings compared to cisgender men.
• Digital & Physical Infrastructure: Access depends heavily on internet connectivity, proximity to banking branches, and digital literacy.
• Legal & Identification Hurdles: Standardized documentation rules can disproportionately affect married women or low-income applicants in specific regional contexts.
