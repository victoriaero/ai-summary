Definition of Use of Force

Use of Force is the amount of effort an officer uses to get a contender to comply with their commands. The law does not dictate how much and when to use force but varies with individual law enforcement agencies. Officers are required to use only the necessary amount of force to complete the arrest or for their protection.

Use of Force Continuum

A Use of Force Continumm is used by many police forces, which is a variety of graded force options that are applied based on the situation.

Guidelines for the use of force are set by the guidelines and have the officers handling situations by going through an increasing sequence of action.

The series of actions starts with verbal commands to actions using deadly force:

  • Officer is present, and no force is used.
  • There are verbal commands given by the officer, which are non-physical.
  • Bodily force is used to gain control of the situation, like grabbing or holding the offender.
  • Less lethal device methods like chemical sprays and CEDs are used by the officer.
  • Finally, as a last resort, the officer uses deadly force.

Excessive Force

Excessive force is when more than necessary force is used to make the arrest. This term is explained further by the Bureau of Justice Statistics; the legal standard for excessive force is if the officer had a reasonable belief to use more force than necessary for the arrest.

Officers are advised to use minimal force, as the opposite can be a violation of the Constitutional rights of the offender. It could also result in a civil or criminal suit against the officer.

Examples and Statistics

A national study has shown that the use of force makes up for only less than 1% of all calls for service. Another study done by the Bureau of Justice Statistics shows that only half of 44 million people who experienced contact with an officer have encountered the use of force.

According to data from The National Police Use of Force Database Project, only about 650 of 150,000 use of force incidents involved excessive force.
