When Disability Discrimination Leads to Mental Health Crises

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. When Disability Discrimination Leads to Mental Health Crises
May 23, 2025Disability Discrimination

Disability discrimination in the workplace can have lasting effects. When employees with disabilities are treated unfairly or denied accommodations, it creates an environment of exclusion and stress. According to the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC), over 25,000 disability discrimination charges were filed in 2023 alone. Such actions often go beyond legal violations and can trigger severe mental health crises. We are here to protect those whose rights have been violated and help them regain their well-being.

Understanding Disability Discrimination’s Impact

Discrimination against employees with disabilities doesn’t just affect their job performance—it affects their mental health. Our Los Angeles disability discrimination lawyer understands that mistreatment in the workplace can lead to increased rates of stress, anxiety, and depression, with studies showing that over 50% of employees with disabilities experience mental health challenges directly linked to workplace mistreatment. The emotional toll can be devastating when employees are subjected to hostile environments or denied necessary support. We fight for employees facing these hardships to ensure compensation for suffering and support in the healing process.

Jeffrey Rager’s Commitment to Disability Discrimination Cases

Jeffrey Rager has been a dedicated advocate for workers facing discrimination, particularly those with disabilities. With years of experience in the field, he recognizes that each case is unique, and the emotional toll can be just as important as the legal outcome. Our Los Angeles disability discrimination attorney, Jeffrey Rager, is committed to understanding clients’ legal and personal challenges. He works tirelessly to secure justice for those whose mental health has been compromised due to workplace mistreatment.

The Role of Rager & Yoon in Protecting Employees

At Rager & Yoon, we are passionate about fighting for workers’ rights. Our legal team specializes in cases where employees have faced discrimination based on their disability. Data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics shows that only 21.3% of people with a disability were employed in 2022, compared to 65.4% of those without a disability, highlighting the systemic barriers disabled individuals face. We recognize the deep emotional and psychological impact that such discrimination can cause. From workplace harassment to wrongful terminations, we are dedicated to securing the compensation and accommodations our clients deserve. Our firm’s comprehensive approach addresses our clients’ legal aspects and mental health recovery.

The Connection Between Mental Health and Discrimination

Disability discrimination can manifest in many ways, including the denial of accommodations, exclusion from key work activities, or overt harassment. These actions are not only harmful in the immediate sense but can also lead to long-term mental health challenges. According to the Job Accommodation Network (JAN), employers can provide most accommodations at no cost (58%) or at a one-time cost of under $500 (37%)—yet many still fail to do so. Anxiety, depression, and burnout can take root when employees are made to feel less than or incapable due to their disability.

Seeking Justice for Mental and Emotional Pain

When an employee’s mental health deteriorates due to discrimination, the emotional pain cannot be ignored. Studies show that workers who face discrimination are more than twice as likely to develop symptoms of depression or anxiety. We approach each case with the understanding that the harm is financial and personal. By securing legal support, employees can start to heal and regain control over their lives. We help our clients navigate the emotional aftermath of discrimination, offering them a chance to move forward with strength and confidence.

The Importance of Legal Action

Taking legal action can be empowering. Not only does it hold employers accountable for their actions, but it also allows employees to reclaim dignity. Legal remedies for disability discrimination can provide financial relief and emotional restoration. In fact, the EEOC reports that monetary benefits obtained through disability discrimination claims exceeded $100 million in 2022, showing the significant impact of legal advocacy. We are committed to ensuring clients are heard and their rights protected, helping restore the peace of mind they deserve.

Conclusion

Disability discrimination in the workplace can lead to devastating mental health consequences, but with the right legal support, you can take steps toward recovery. If you are struggling with the effects of discrimination, we are here to help.
