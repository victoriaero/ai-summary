Administrative Burdens and Racial Disparities in Economic Security Following Job Loss

1 Sep 2023

This study examines the impact of administrative burdens on income transfer receipt among Black, Latino, and White families after a job loss, using data from the Current Population Survey (1990-2019). The research finds that administrative burdens have increased for TANF and Unemployment Insurance, while decreasing for SNAP. These burdens contribute to lower income replacement rates for Black and Latino families compared to White families, with effects varying based on state demographics. The findings suggest that reducing administrative hurdles in income transfer programs could mitigate racial and ethnic inequalities in economic security.
