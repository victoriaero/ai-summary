Information and data on the mass criminalization and incarceration of LGBTQ+ people
On this page, the Prison Policy Initiative has curated all of the research about LGBT+ issues in the criminal justice system that we know of. You can also see a selection of our best original research on this topic on our Women and gender page. For research on other criminal legal system topics, see our Research Library homepage.
LGBTQ people are overrepresented at every stage of the criminal legal system, starting with juvenile justice system involvement. The research below highlights not only how police disproportionately target LGTBQ people, particularly queer women and trans people, but also the myriad of ways that they are discriminated against and victimized while incarcerated.
