Clear and comprehensive job descriptions help candidates self assess their fit for a role, leading to higher quality applications and reducing time spent screening irrelevant candidates during the selection process.

Before accepting any offer, job seekers should reflect on their career values and personal goals to ensure the company's culture and growth opportunities truly align with what they want from their career.
