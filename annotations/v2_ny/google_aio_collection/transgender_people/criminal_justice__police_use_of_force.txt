• Race and Intersectionality
	• Black and people of color transgender individuals experience physical force and violence by police at significantly higher rates than white transgender people.

• Profiling and Sex Work Assumptions
	• Police often assume that transgender people in public spaces are engaging in sex work.
	• This profiling leads to aggressive stops, searches, and a higher risk of physical force.
• Gender Bias and Transphobia
	• Officers frequently view gender nonconformity as deviant or deceptive.
	• This bias triggers hostile attitudes, insulting language, and escalated conflicts.
• Socioeconomic Factors
	• High rates of homelessness and poverty force many transgender individuals to spend time in public areas.
