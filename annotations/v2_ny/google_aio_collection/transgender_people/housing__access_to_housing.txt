Several key systemic, financial, and legal factors significantly influence housing access and stability for transgender people.
Discrimination and Harassment
• Rental and Housing Denials: According to data compiled by organizations like Advocates for Trans Equality, roughly 1 in 5 transgender individuals report being denied an apartment or home due to their gender identity or expression.
• Eviction Rates: Approximately 10% to 11% of transgender people report facing eviction specifically because of their gender identity or expression.
• Shelter Barriers: When seeking emergency or temporary housing, transgender individuals frequently face harassment, denial of entry, or dangerous placements that misalign with their gender identity, according to reports from the National Alliance to End Homelessness.
Financial and Economic Insecurity
• Income and Employment Disparities: Widespread workplace discrimination limits employment opportunities, directly contributing to higher rates of poverty and severe rent burdens.
• Homeownership Gaps: Transgender adults experience substantially lower homeownership rates (estimated around 25%) compared to cisgender adults (around 58%), driven by difficulties in securing stable income and saving for down payments.
• Family Rejection: Rejection by family members—particularly affecting LGBTQ+ youth—leads to premature displacement, higher rates of youth homelessness, and interrupted education or career paths.
Legal and Policy Frameworks
• Identity Documents: Mismatched names or gender markers on official identification documents create administrative roadblocks when applying for housing, public assistance, or formal leases.
• Enforcement of Protections: While the federal Fair Housing Act prohibits sex discrimination (which has at times been interpreted to cover gender identity), shifting federal directives and enforcement practices by agencies like the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) create an unstable patchwork of protections, leaving enforcement heavily dependent on state and local laws.
