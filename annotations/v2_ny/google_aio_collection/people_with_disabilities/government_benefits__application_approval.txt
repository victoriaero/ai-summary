Key factors influencing government disability benefit approvals include income limits, medical evidence severity, and your work history.
Government agencies like the Social Security Administration (SSA) use a multi-step evaluation to decide who gets benefits.
📊 Financial & Work Limits
• Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA): Earning above the monthly limit—$1,690 in 2026—generally disqualifies you.
• Work Credits: You must have enough recent work credits based on your age and earnings for programs like SSDI.
🏥 Medical Documentation
• Blue Book Listings: Conditions matching official SSA Blue Book criteria speed up approval.
• Duration Requirement: The condition must last or be expected to last at least 12 months or result in death.
• Treatment Consistency: Gaps in treatment or failure to follow doctor orders can lead to a denial.
🧑‍💼 Vocational Factors
• Age: Applicants over age 50 face fewer job retraining expectations under SSA Medical-Vocational Guidelines.
• Past Work: The SSA evaluates your residual functional capacity to see if you can do past jobs or adjust to other work in the national economy.
