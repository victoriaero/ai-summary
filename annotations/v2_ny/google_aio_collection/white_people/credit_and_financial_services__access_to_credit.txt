	• Higher baseline homeownership (approx. 75% for White households).

	• Higher average starting credit scores in majority-White communities (median ~687–730).

• 📍 Geographic and Neighborhood Context
	• Residency in areas with high upward economic mobility and established financial infrastructure.
	• Proximity to traditional brick-and-mortar bank branches.
