Housing stability for White households is primarily influenced by higher rates of generational wealth, advantageous access to mortgage credit, and historical policy advantages that drive high homeownership rates.
📊 Key Economic & Structural Factors
• 💵 Wealth and Inheritance: High baseline levels of generational wealth and inheritance gaps facilitate down payments and buffer against income shocks.
• 🏦 Mortgage Access: Favorable lending terms and lower rates of mortgage discrimination support sustained long-term homeownership.
• 🏡 Property Appreciation: Higher baseline neighborhood appreciation and equitable property valuations build housing equity over time.
• 📜 Historical Policy: Cumulative benefits from mid-20th-century housing policies (like federally backed mortgages and suburban development) continue to reinforce stability.
• 🗺️ Zoning and Local Control: Single-family zoning laws often protect property values and neighborhood resource allocation for existing homeowners.
