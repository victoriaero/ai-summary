AI OVERVIEW TEXT

Socioeconomic status, geographic location, and digital infrastructure are the primary factors influencing access to banking and financial services for non-Latino populations.
📊 Key Factors Influencing Financial Access
• Income Levels: Low household income and insufficient funds to meet bank minimum balance requirements create primary barriers.
• Geographic Proximity: Rural residence and physical distance from brick-and-mortar bank branches limit traditional banking access.
• Digital Connectivity: Reliable internet access reduces involuntary financial exclusion by enabling online and mobile banking.
• Educational Attainment: Lower financial literacy and education levels correlate with higher rates of financial exclusion.
• Neighborhood Demographics: Localized socioeconomic conditions and community-level economic disinvestment impact overall service availability.
