📊 Core Financial Factors
• Credit Score: Evaluates payment history, credit utilization, and length of credit history.

• Income and Employment: Assesses the stability and sufficiency of earnings.
• Collateral or Down Payment: Measures asset backing relative to the loan size.
• Credit History Depth: Looks at past performance on installment loans, revolving lines, and mortgages.
🗒 Operational & Contextual Variables
• Leverage Ratio: Compares total debt or loan amount to asset value or property value.
• Documentation Verification: Confirms asset ownership, tax returns, and liquid reserves.
