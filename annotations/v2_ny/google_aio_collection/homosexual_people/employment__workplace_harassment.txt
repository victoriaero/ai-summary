• Intersectionality with Race and Gender: Rates of harassment are significantly higher for LGBTQ+ employees of color (BIPOC) and transgender or nonbinary individuals compared to their white or cisgender peers.
• Cultural and Religious Beliefs: A substantial portion of workplace mistreatment is attributed by employees to co-workers' or supervisors' religious and moral objections to homosexuality.
• Organizational Climate and Policies: The absence of explicit non-discrimination protections, weak diversity training regarding hidden stigmas, and poor enforcement mechanisms by management or HR foster environments where offensive "jokes" and banter go unchecked.
