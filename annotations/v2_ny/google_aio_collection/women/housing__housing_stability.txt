📊 Economic Security and Affordability
• Income Disparities: The persistent gender wage gap limits women's overall purchasing power.
• Rent Burden: High proportions of income spent on rent drive severe housing cost stress.
• Single Parenthood: Single-mother households face exponentially higher poverty and financial strain rates.
• Rental Arrears: Debt accumulation directly escalates eviction risks, disproportionately hitting women of color.
⚠️ Gender-Based and Intimate Partner Violence (IPV)
• Abuse as a Driver: Domestic violence remains a leading structural cause of female homelessness.
• Barriers to Leaving: A lack of safe, affordable housing options forces survivors to choose between staying with abusers or displacement.
• Compromised Records: Abusers frequently sabotage credit scores or rental histories, blocking future independent housing access.
💡 Systemic and Structural Disparities
• Eviction Rates: Women experience statistically higher eviction filing and execution rates than men.
• Discrimination: Unequal property rights, biased lending practices, and historical "pinklining" restrict fair access.
• Intersectional Vulnerabilities: Gender-expansive individuals, undocumented survivors, and women with disabilities face compounded exclusion.
🔎 Psychosocial and Ontological Security
• Sense of Home: Stability requires emotional safety, community connection, and comfort—not just physical shelter.
• Mental Health: Chronic housing instability correlates tightly with anxiety, PTSD symptoms, and psychological distress.
