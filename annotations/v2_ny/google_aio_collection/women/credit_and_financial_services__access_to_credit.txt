Access to credit for women is heavily influenced by systemic property disparities, traditional collateral rules, and historical gaps in financial inclusion.
🏠 Collateral and Property Rights
• Unequal land and property ownership restrict traditional loan approval.
• Lenders frequently demand fixed immovable assets as collateral.
• Movable collateral systems help mitigate this specific barrier.
📊 Credit History and Scoring
• Shorter or missing formal credit histories lead to higher risk perceptions.
• Disparities in formal earnings affect baseline scoring models.
• Alternative credit scoring via utility data expands access.
🌐 Literacy and Digital Access
• Lower digital and financial literacy hinder platform navigation.
• Higher transaction and mobility costs reduce engagement.
• Digital financial services reduce delivery friction.
⚖️ Cultural and Legal Frameworks
• Prevailing social norms influence independent financial decision-making.
• Historical legal exclusions delayed independent account creation until the mid-1970s.
• Explicit anti-discrimination policies improve borrowing parity.
