Credit and loan approval decisions rely primarily on objective financial metrics that evaluate an applicant's capacity and willingness to repay debt.
📊 Key Financial Factors
• 
📌 Payment History (35%)

	• Tracks timely repayment of past credit lines and loans.
	• Late payments or defaults significantly lower approval odds.
• 
📌 Credit Utilization Ratio (Under 30%)

	• Measures revolving credit used versus total available limit.
	• Lower utilization (ideally under 10%) signals prudent debt management.
• 
📌 Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)

	• Compares monthly debt obligations to gross monthly income.
	• Lenders typically prefer a DTI ratio below 36% to 43%.
• 
📌 Credit History Length (15%)

	• Reflects the age of oldest and average open accounts.
	• Longer histories provide deeper behavioral data for risk models.
• 
📌 Income and Employment Stability

	• Verifies a steady, reliable cash flow to service new debt.
	• Consistent job duration strengthens financial capacity proof.
