Access to traditional banking and credit services for individuals without disabilities is primarily shaped by socioeconomic status, credit history, and technological or physical proximity to financial institutions.
📊 Economic & Income Factors
Financial stability and account costs dictate whether consumers can open or maintain traditional bank accounts.
• Low or fluctuating monthly income levels
• High minimum balance requirements
• Costly monthly maintenance and overdraft fees
⚠️ Credit History & Background
Underwriting standards and past banking records restrict access to mainstream credit and checking products.
• Absence of an established credit score
• Past negative banking history or unpaid negative balances
• Lack of access to a qualified co-signer
💡 Technological & Geographic Barriers
Infrastructure limitations prevent everyday engagement with modern digital and physical banking systems.
• Geographic distance from physical bank branches
• Poor internet access or lack of smartphones
• Language barriers or low financial literacy levels
