Credit and loan approvals depend on a combination of your credit score history, income stability, and overall debt load. [1, 2] 
## ✅ Credit Score and History

* Payment history:
* Accounts for 35% of a typical FICO score.
   * Tracks past on-time payments versus defaults. [3, 4] 
* Credit utilization:
* Accounts for 30% of your score.
   * Ratio of current revolving debt to total limits; ideally under 30%. [3, 4] 
* Length of credit history:
* Accounts for 15% of your score.
   * Longer active histories show proven reliability. [3, 4, 5] 

## 📊 Income and Employment Stability

* Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI):
* Compares total monthly debt payments to gross monthly income.
   * Lenders typically look for a DTI below 35% to 50%. [2, 6] 
* Employment history:
* Verifies 2+ years of steady job tenure or reliable self-employment.
   * Confirms regular cash flow to service new debt. [2, 7] 
* Income level and verification:
* Evaluates gross and net earnings via pay stubs or tax returns.
   * Ensures adequate disposable income after existing obligations. [7, 8] 

## 💡 Collateral and Loan Structure

* Collateral value:
* Secured loans require physical assets like real estate or vehicles.
   * Mitigates lender risk through asset seizure rights. [2] 
* Down payment / Margin money:
* Reduces the loan-to-value (LTV) ratio.
   * Demonstrates borrower skin-in-the-game. [8] 
* Loan amount and term:
* Shorter repayment windows improve approval odds.
   * Larger amounts demand stricter asset and income proof. [7, 8]
