Final-paper script set
======================

RUN.py
RUN.sh
    Orchestrates Dallas (v1_dallas), NYC (v2_ny), and LA (v3_la).

consistency.py
    Original collection validator from the uploaded scripts folder.

link_count_analysis.py
    Based on test_links.py.
    Made location-configurable and added 20,000 paired-outcome bootstrap CIs
    to focal-vs-comparison dimension and aggregate effects.

source_overlap_analysis.py
    Based on links_quadrant.py.
    Made location-configurable; retains sign-flip tests, bootstrap CIs,
    Wilcoxon checks, effect sizes, Holm correction, and source-set diagnostics.

semantic_displacement_analysis.py
    Based on embedding.py.
    Made location-configurable; retains primary, robustness, sentence-level,
    global, and post-hoc analyses.

collect_cited_sources.py
    Based on coletalinks.py.
    Made location-configurable; remains resumable and stores a separate source
    corpus for each collection version.

evidence_synthesis_analysis.py
    Based on evidence_synthesis.py.
    Made location-configurable; reads the matching overlap/semantic results for
    each city and retains 40–80% coverage sensitivity plus blocked correlations.

query_selection_agreement.py
    Based on gwet.py.
    Adds machine-readable agreement_summary.json and selected-set descriptives.

summarize_three_locations.py
    New convenience script collecting the primary per-city effect estimates,
    CIs, corrected p-values, collection quality, and pipeline correlations.

pooled_three_location_secondary.py
    New OPTIONAL secondary pooled robustness analysis. It averages geographic
    repetitions within outcome before inference so the three locations are not
    treated as independent outcomes. It is not a replacement for the primary
    per-location analysis.
